04 — Philosophy

Yoga is a way to know the body, not to chase a perfect posture. I adjust for each student’s body and strength, and I want the practice to release stress, relax, and build body awareness.

How I teach

“Know yourself. Respect the body. Progress step by step.”

My view of yoga

“Yoga is a course you keep practicing for a lifetime.”

There is no finish line. Postures teach the body and breathing builds focus. RYT 200 is a start, and I want students to release stress and know themselves better.

How I work with people

  • Patient
  • Attentive
  • Empathetic

I put safety first and meet each student openly, so they can grow at their own pace.
